How It Works

Three steps. No surprises.

01

We walk the space

Call or book online and we'll set a time, often within a day or two. We walk through and look for anything you're tossing that we can put cash on, before discussing removal at all.

02

You get a cash offer

If we find items worth buying, we make an offer right there. No back-and-forth, no waiting on an appraisal. You decide on the spot, no pressure.

03

We clear the rest

Once the buying side is settled, we load and remove everything else. Spaces are left swept and ready, whether that's a room, a garage, or a whole property.
