On-Site Buying Program

Standard practice: offer 10–20 cents on the resale dollar. The discount comes off the customer's bill today; the margin (resale value minus what you offered) is realized when you sell the item, typically within 2–4 weeks, minus eBay/Mercari/Facebook platform and final value fees (typically 12–15% of the sale price).
