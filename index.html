The process

Three steps. Property cleared.

01

Call, text, or book online

Tell us what you've got — a few sentences or photos. We confirm a time, usually same-day or next-day, and give you a ballpark quote upfront.

02

We arrive, assess, and pay you

We walk the property, identify anything of value, and put a cash offer on the table — before a single item moves. Accept or decline, no obligation.

03

Everything gone, done

We load, haul, and handle disposal. You get before/after photo documentation, a signed receipt, and a clear property. Payment on completion.
