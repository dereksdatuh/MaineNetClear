Common questions

Junk removal and buying program, explained

Straight answers about how a job actually works, from first call to final sweep.

Is Maine Net Clear a junk removal company?

Yes. We're a full-service junk removal and property clearing company serving all of York County. We haul furniture, appliances, debris, and general property contents of any size, from a single item to a full estate. The difference is what happens before the truck gets loaded.

How does the buying program lower my cost?

Before we remove anything, we walk the property and make cash offers on items of resale value, tools, furniture, collectibles, antiques, electronics, and more. Whatever we buy is paid to you on the spot and subtracted from your total removal cost. Most customers see real savings on their final bill.

Do I need to sell anything to use your removal service?

No. The buying program is always optional. If we make an offer on something, you can accept or decline with no obligation. Either way, we still remove and dispose of everything you need cleared.

How fast can you get here?

Most jobs are scheduled same-day or next-day. Call, text, or submit a quote request with a few details and photos, and we'll confirm a time window that works for your schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Kennebunk and serve all of York County, including Kennebunkport, Wells, Ogunquit, York, Biddeford, Saco, Old Orchard Beach, Scarborough, Sanford, South Berwick, Eliot, Kittery, Arundel, Buxton, and Waterboro.
